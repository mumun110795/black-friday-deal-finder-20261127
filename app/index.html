How Cartwise checks a deal. We read each price at the store itself or in a deals source that names the store, on the morning stamped on every card, and we print the source beside the price. The discount on a card is always the discount the two prices on that card imply — no rounded-up percentages, no prices from before Black Friday week. A verdict is our honest call: a new low, a strong price, or a routine sale wearing a Black Friday badge.

Prices were live when checked and can move; the store page is the final word. Cartwise is an independent deal finder and is not affiliated with any store it lists.
